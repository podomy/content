### Reviews

Academic or engineering paper reviews align with the following steps:

1. The Abstract & Claims (10-min skim)
2. The Critical Flaw / Friction Point (Where does it fail in production / violate our principles?)
3. The Extraction (Extract the 10% pure algorithmic gold; discard 90% bloat)
4. Markdown Teardown + C Prototype + TLA+ mathematically verified design.
